Began a research placement as **AI Research Assistant** at the **Université de Moncton**,
New Brunswick, Canada, supported by a Mitacs Globalink Research Award. The placement ran
from March to August 2026.

The work was **generative quantum machine learning for drug discovery**: a literature
review covering molecular generative models and variational quantum circuits; a curated
dataset of 5-lipoxygenase (5-LOX) inhibitors drawn from ChEMBL, PubChem and BindingDB;
and hybrid quantum–classical generative architectures (QGAN, QVAE) and Transformers
implemented and evaluated for the *de novo* generation of 5-LOX inhibitors.
