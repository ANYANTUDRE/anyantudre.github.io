Received the **Mitacs Globalink Research Award (GRA)**, a competitive **$12,000 CAD**
award supporting a research project carried out at a Canadian university under joint
supervision.

The award funded the placement at the **Université de Moncton**, on quantum machine
learning for drug discovery.
