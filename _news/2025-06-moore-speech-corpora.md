Released **MooreSpeechCorpora**, an open-source toolkit to collect, preprocess, align and
normalise low-resource **Mooré** speech and text data. Mooré is spoken by more than
7 million people across five or more West African countries.

The toolkit automates the scraping of parallel text–audio data, resamples and cleans the
audio, runs forced alignment with the MMS Forced Aligner, and exports straight to the
Hugging Face Datasets format.
