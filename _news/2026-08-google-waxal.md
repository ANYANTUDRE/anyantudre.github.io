Finished **2nd out of 242 teams** in the **Google WAXAL ASR Challenge**, and was the
**best African participant**. The prize was **$4,000 USD**.

The task was multilingual automatic speech recognition for three low-resource African
languages. The challenge was organised by Google Research and hosted on Zindi.
