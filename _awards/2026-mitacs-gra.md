Received the **Mitacs Globalink Research Award (GRA)**, a competitive **$12,000 CAD** award supporting a research project at a Canadian university — in this case **quantum machine learning** at Université de Moncton.

## About the Award

The Globalink Research Award provides financial support for a research project carried out at a Canadian university under joint supervision.

## Research Focus

- Quantum machine learning for drug discovery
- Hybrid quantum-classical generative models
- Hosted by Université de Moncton, Canada
