Shape, from the approved design's `post` view: a FULL-WIDTH BAND carrying the rubric, the title, the standfirst and the byline, closed by a spectrogram rule; then the prose column with the right margin column beside it holding the metadata and the links; then the related projects. Every word of it comes from the item's own front matter — title, excerpt, date, image, tags, github_url, github_stars. Nothing is written here. The page is data-view="work" (set in _config.yml's portfolio defaults), so the navbar marks Work, not Writing, while the reader is inside a project.
