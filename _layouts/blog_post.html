--- layout: redesign-default --- {%- comment -%} THE POST — the design's `post` view, ported. A full-width band carries the rubric, the title, the dek and the byline; neither the title nor the dek declares a width, so a title that fits on one line stays on one line and text-wrap:balance is the only thing allowed to break it. Under the band the page becomes three columns: the apparatus on the left (sticky from 1300px up), the prose in the middle, and the right rail carrying the outline — which hangs off a zero-height sticky anchor and therefore costs no line box — together with any sidenotes. `view: post` comes from the posts defaults in _config.yml, so the shell stamps data-view="post" on : that widens --gap to 4rem at 960px, wins the post its own left lane at 1300px, and marks Writing in the navbar. Everything on this page is read from the post's own front matter. The hero is page.image.path, sitting directly under the band and capped to the prose column by .col so it can never overflow into the rail. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign words = content | strip_html | number_of_words -%} {%- assign mins = words | divided_by: site.words_per_minute -%} {%- if mins < 1 %}{% assign mins = 1 %}{% endif -%} {%- assign cat = page.categories[0] -%} {%- assign cat_label = cat | replace: '_', ' ' | replace: '-', ' ' -%} {%- assign hits = page.url | absolute_url | url_encode -%} {%- comment -%} The shell stamps site.locale on , so the French twin has to declare its own language on the three elements that actually hold French — the title, the dek and the prose — or a screen reader reads them in an English voice.
