ROUND 6, § 1. The rule is DERIVED FROM THE PERMALINK, never typed, so it cannot drift as pages are added: the French counterpart of /x/ is /fr/x/ and the English counterpart of /fr/x/ is /x/. `site.pages | where: 'url'` then asks whether that page actually exists in this build — so a route with no counterpart can never produce a 404. Three cases, in order of authority: 1. translation_en / translation_fr in front matter WINS. Blog posts are written twice rather than translated once and already carry the pair; that mechanism is untouched, this rule simply defers to it. 2. A counterpart page exists at the mirrored permalink — link to it. 3. Nothing mirrors this page (a project, an award, a milestone, a legacy route). The link then goes to the French INDEX for the section the reader is in, read from page.view, which is exactly where a reader who asked for French wants to land.
