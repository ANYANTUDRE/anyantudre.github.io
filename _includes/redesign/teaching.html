{%- comment -%} THE TEACHING VIEW — the design's `talks` page. Two sections, each a heading at the container's left edge, its spectrogram rule directly under it, and the CONTENT stepped in by --setback inside .sb. Everything comes from _teaching and _talks: title, one-sentence excerpt, venue, date and location are each item's own front matter. Awards and certifications do not live here — they are on the CV and on /certifications/.
