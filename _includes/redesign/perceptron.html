One unit, three inputs

y = φ( ∑i wixi + b )

[one artificial neuron, Rosenblatt 1958, The Perceptron]

The three inputs are the three things I actually work on. The weights are where the hours went — speech has been carrying the largest one for two years now. The bias is everything else: the teaching, the talks, and the reading that never reaches a repository. The activation is what any of it is worth to somebody who is not me.
